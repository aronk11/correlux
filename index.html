04 / FAQ

Operational details.

Is this a K9s replacement?+

Correlux is inspired by K9s, with a different starting point: applications and their health. Use it when you want connected evidence and a path from “something is broken” to “I know why.” It is still early software; the roadmap is public.

Does anything run inside my cluster?+

No additional component. Correlux talks to the Kubernetes API from your terminal using your existing credentials and permissions. There is no agent, server or CRD to deploy. Live resource usage optionally uses the metrics API.

Is the WHY engine powered by AI?+

No. Thirteen deterministic rules inspect evidence from your cluster. Findings include the evidence, confidence and what to check next. When the evidence cannot establish a cause, Correlux says so.

Can it change production resources?+

Yes. Scale and edit are available, with explicit confirmation; edits show a diff. Contexts identified as production require their name by default. Interactive shells also require production confirmation. Kubernetes RBAC still controls what you can do. The fleet overview is read-only.

How mature is Correlux?+

Early and actively evolving. Applications, diagnosis, resource browsing, logs, fleet groups, usage, scale and edit are implemented. Delete, restart and cordon are planned. See the release notes for your version and the development roadmap for what comes next.
